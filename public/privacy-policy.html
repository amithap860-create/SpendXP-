Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026  ·  Effective immediately

What we collect

Email, country, age group, and newsletter preference — only what you give us.

Why we collect it

To notify you when SpendXP launches and send you optional updates.

Who sees it

Only SpendXP and our form tool (Tally.so). We never sell your data.

Your rights

You can request to view, correct, or delete your data at any time.

1Who We Are

SpendXP is a financial literacy app designed to teach young people (ages 8 and up) how to manage, save, and understand money through quests, ranks, and real-life financial challenges.

We are currently in pre-launch and collecting a waitlist ahead of our August 2026 release. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information submitted through our waitlist form.

Contact: spendxp.app@gmail.com

2Information We Collect

We only collect the information you voluntarily submit through our waitlist form. This includes:

Data Point What It Is Why We Need It
Email address Your email To notify you when SpendXP launches
Country The country you're based in To ensure availability in your region at launch
Age group 8–12, 13–15, 16–18, or 19–20 To tailor content and comply with age-related legal requirements
Newsletter preference Yes / No To know if you'd like to receive updates about SpendXP development

We do not collect payment information, identification documents, school details, or any sensitive personal data through this form.

3How We Use Your Information

  • To send you a notification when SpendXP launches in your region
  • To send development updates and newsletters if you opted in
  • To understand which age groups and countries are most interested in SpendXP
  • To improve our app before launch based on interest signals

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal information to any third party for marketing purposes.

4Children's Privacy

Important — for users under 13: If you are under 13 years old, please ask a parent or guardian to complete this form on your behalf, or to review this policy and give their consent before you submit your information.

SpendXP is designed for users as young as age 8. We take children's privacy very seriously and comply with applicable laws including India's Information Technology (Reasonable Security Practices) Rules and, where applicable, the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA).

  • We only collect the minimum information necessary — no full name, school, location beyond country, or any detailed profile
  • We do not display advertising to children on our waitlist or in our app
  • Parents or guardians may contact us at any time to request deletion of their child's information
  • We do not share children's data with any third party except our form service (Tally.so) as described below

5Third-Party Services

Our waitlist form is powered by Tally.so. When you submit the form, your information is stored on Tally's servers. Tally's own Privacy Policy applies to how they store and process this data — you can read it at tally.so/privacy.

SpendXP's main application (launching August 2026) is built with Firebase (Google). Once the app launches, a separate in-app privacy policy will apply to registered users.

We do not use advertising networks, sell data, or share your information with any analytics companies. The only data processor involved at this stage is Tally.so for form submission storage.

6How Long We Keep Your Data

We will retain your waitlist information until one of the following occurs:

  • SpendXP launches and we have sent you the notification you signed up for
  • You request deletion of your information (see Your Rights below)
  • We decide not to proceed with the SpendXP launch, in which case we will delete all waitlist data within 30 days of that decision

If you opted in to the newsletter, we will continue to store your email for the purpose of sending updates until you unsubscribe.

7Your Rights

Regardless of where you are located, you have the right to:

  • Access — Request a copy of the information we hold about you
  • Correct — Ask us to update inaccurate information
  • Delete — Request that we delete your information from our records and from Tally
  • Withdraw consent — Unsubscribe from newsletters at any time via the unsubscribe link in any email we send
  • Complain — If you believe we have mishandled your data, you may contact your local data protection authority

To exercise any of these rights, email us at amithap860@gmail.com with the subject line "Privacy Request." We will respond within 14 days.

8Security

We take reasonable precautions to protect your information. Your form data is stored by Tally.so, which uses industry-standard encryption (HTTPS/TLS) for data in transit. We access the data only via Tally's secure dashboard.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is 100% secure. While we strive to protect your data, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9Changes to This Policy

As SpendXP grows, this policy may be updated. If we make material changes, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

If you have joined our newsletter, we may also notify you of significant changes via email.
