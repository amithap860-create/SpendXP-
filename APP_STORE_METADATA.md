```
SpendXP turns financial literacy into a daily adventure.

Join the Order of the Golden Ledger — a secret organisation battling financial ignorance across 7 districts. Earn XP, climb through 8 ranks, and unlock the skills that textbooks don't teach.

──── WHAT YOU'LL LEARN ────

• Budgeting & spending wisely
• Saving goals and compound interest

Case Files (Quests)
Complete scenario-based challenges — negotiate a salary, balance a budget, invest a windfall. Each Case File teaches a real concept and rewards XP.

Arcade Games
Test your skills in fast, fun mini-games:
• Budget Blitz — make snap spending decisions under pressure
• FinIQ Quiz — how well do you know money?

Daily Streak
Log in every day to build your streak. Streak Shields protect you on off days. The habit of thinking about money daily is itself a superpower.

Rank System
Progress from Apprentice all the way to Legend. Each rank unlocks new story chapters, districts, and challenges.

──── FOR FAMILIES ────

Parents can connect to their child's account to track learning progress, see concept proficiency, and set healthy daily time limits. The Order has rules — fair play for everyone.

──── PRIVACY FIRST ────

SpendXP is built for young learners. We comply with COPPA (under-13 parental consent flow) and never sell your data. No ads. Ever.

Free: 3 quests per day, all three arcade games, full rank progression.
Premium (Agent tier): unlimited quests, Stock Market Simulator, Credit Score Builder, Streak Shield, exclusive avatars, deep analytics, and early access to new features. Pricing coming soon — join the waitlist in the app.

Join over [X] learners already building smarter money habits.
```

## App Store Screenshots Guide

You need screenshots for each device size. Shoot in the simulator or on device.

### Required sizes (iOS)
| Device | Size |
|---|---|
| iPhone 6.9" (Pro Max) | 1320 × 2868 px |
| iPhone 6.5" (older Max) | 1242 × 2688 px |
| iPad 12.9" (if supporting iPad) | 2048 × 2732 px |

### Screenshot order & what to show

1. **Dashboard / Hero** — Show the rank banner ("Apprentice of the Golden Ledger"), XP bar, streak counter, and today's active Case File. Caption: *"Level up your money IQ every day"*

2. **Case Files (Quests)** — Show the quest list with a Case File open (the narrative intro screen with the "Accept the Assignment" button). Caption: *"Real-world scenarios, real financial skills"*

3. **Games Hub** — Show the 3 free games + the 2 premium locked cards (blurred/locked). Caption: *"Earn XP through fast, fun challenges"*

4. **Budget Blitz or FinIQ Quiz in action** — Show mid-game with the timer and score. Caption: *"Think fast — your budget won't wait"*

5. **Profile / Rank Progress** — Show the avatar, current rank, XP progress ring, streak count, and the "Order of the Golden Ledger" section. Caption: *"Track every milestone, earn every rank"*

6. **Parent Dashboard** — Show the concept proficiency radar chart and recent activity feed. Caption: *"Parents stay in the loop — effortlessly"*

### Screenshot tips
- Use dark navy background screens (Dashboard, Games) for the most striking shots
- Show a character who is a few ranks in (e.g. "Scout" rank) so the progression looks real
- The narrative/quest opening screens are very unique — show them prominently
- Use a real device if possible (not just simulator) for the most polished screenshots
