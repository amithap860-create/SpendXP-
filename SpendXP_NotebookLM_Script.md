## What is SpendXP?

SpendXP is a financial literacy app that teaches teenagers and young adults how to manage money — not through boring lectures, but through a game. Users earn XP (experience points) for completing financial challenges, play arcade games that simulate real money decisions, and climb a rank system inspired by secret order mythology.

The core idea: money skills should feel as engaging as a video game. Every decision in the app mirrors a real-world financial scenario — first paycheck, credit card traps, EMI loans, emergency funds, stock market basics.

---

## The Problem SpendXP Solves

Most young people reach adulthood with zero financial education. Schools don't teach it. Parents often don't know enough to pass it on. By the time they get their first salary, they're already making mistakes — overspending, ignoring savings, not understanding credit scores.

SpendXP puts financial education in the pocket of every student, in a format they actually want to open every day.

---

## How It Works — The Core Loop

1. **Case Files (Quests)** — The main learning mode. Each "Case File" is a real financial scenario: "Your first apartment," "Your first credit card," "Group chat dilemma — do you split the bill?" Users read the scenario, answer questions, and earn XP. Every case teaches one concrete financial skill.

2. **Arcade Games** — Three free games reinforce concepts through play:
   - **Budget Blitz** — Sort expenses into needs, wants, and savings as fast as you can.
   - **FinIQ Quiz** — Daily scenarios that test your financial knowledge. A new quiz every day.
   - **Money Maze** — Puzzle-based game where you allocate money across competing priorities.

3. **Premium Games** — Unlock with a subscription:
   - **Stock Market Simulator** — Trade virtual stocks based on simulated news. Learn investing without risk.
   - **Credit Score Builder** — Make lifecycle choices and watch your credit score rise or fall.

4. **XP and Ranks** — Every activity earns XP. XP unlocks ranks: Apprentice → Scout → Investor → Analyst → Strategist → Grandmaster → Legend. Each rank has a storyline, a district name, and a "Gray Fog enemy" — a metaphor for the financial bad habit that rank trains you to defeat (Impulse Storm, Debt Mirage, Inflation Phantom, etc.).

5. **Daily Streak** — Log in and complete at least one activity every day to maintain your streak. Missing a day resets it. (Premium users get a Streak Shield once a week.)

## The Storyline — Order of the Golden Ledger

SpendXP wraps its financial education in a narrative: the user is a recruit in a secret organization called the Order of the Golden Ledger. The Order fights "the Gray Fog" — a metaphor for financial ignorance and bad money habits.

Each rank is a promotion within the Order. Each Case File is a "mission." Each game is a "training exercise." This narrative makes the learning feel purposeful and gives users a reason to keep coming back.

---

## Who It's For

- **Primary audience:** 14–25 year olds who have never been taught money management
- **Secondary:** Parents who want to introduce financial concepts to their kids in a fun way
- **Tertiary:** Employers or schools looking for financial literacy tools

The app adapts content to three age groups: Junior (under 16), Teen (16–20), Senior (20+). Younger users get simpler language and analogies. Older users get the full complexity.

## Key Concepts Taught

1. Budgeting basics (needs vs. wants vs. savings)
2. Emergency funds — why and how much
3. Credit scores — how they work, how to build them
4. EMI and debt — how installment traps work
5. Investing basics — stocks, compound interest, diversification
6. First paycheck — tax deductions, take-home vs. gross
7. Side hustles — pricing, profit margin, tax basics
8. Renting vs. buying — first apartment decisions
9. Phone plans and subscriptions — hidden cost traps
10. Group expense management — splitting bills, social pressure spending
11. Birthday spending — peer pressure and impulse buying
